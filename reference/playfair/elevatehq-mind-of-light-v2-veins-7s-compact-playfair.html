Mind of Light, version 2 compact with the Playfair Display logo

A live 3D scene drawn by your browser’s graphics chip. It is not a video, so it stays sharp at any size and reacts to the visitor.

0–1.6 s

A universe of thoughts

Thousands of points of light swirl like a galaxy: everything a person carries in their head.

1.6–3.6 s

One mind

The galaxy collapses into a 3D brain made of glowing veins that grow from the brain stem, built from the brain in your logo. It turns to follow the mouse.

3.6–5 s

A spark

Gold light flows outward through every vein like a thought travelling, and the leaf’s own veins fill with light: growth beginning.

5–7 s

Elevated

The brain turns to face you, dissolves, and the light re-forms into your exact logo. A faint shimmer stays alive behind it.
